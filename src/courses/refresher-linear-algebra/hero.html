Refresher Course — Linear Algebra

This is the linear algebra block of the USI Refresher Course in Mathematics and Probability, rebuilt as a course you can actually read. It follows the five lectures exactly — Matrix terminology and matrix algebra, Vector spaces, Geometry of matrices, Systems of linear equations — but fills in everything the slides leave to the lecturer: what each definition is really saying, why it is stated that way, and how the pieces connect.

Every exercise that appears on a slide is here with a complete solution, including the ones whose answer slide was left blank, plus the seven problems from the separate Exercises on Matrices sheet. That includes the finance exercises the lecturer added: the covariance matrix, the budget set, fully-invested versus self-financing portfolios, replication and arbitrage, market completeness, and the Arrow–Debreu state-price problem.

How to use: modules 01–04 are matrix mechanics — read them in order, they are short. Modules 05–08 are the abstract core (vector space, span, independence, basis); they are the ones that make the rest easy, so do not skim them. Modules 09–11 turn those ideas into three computable numbers (rank, determinant, inverse). Modules 12–13 answer the question the whole block is built for: when does $A\mathbf{x}=\mathbf{b}$ have a solution, is it unique, and how do you find it. Module 14 collects all 30 exercises with worked solutions.
