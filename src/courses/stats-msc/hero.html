Statistics — MSc (S. Peluso)

The MSc statistics course, rebuilt lecture by lecture. The handwritten lecture notes come first: modules 01–15 follow lectures 1 and 2 page by page — every example the lecturer worked on the board is here, solved step by step, with a picture next to it. The six PDF decks (the "PDF notes" the lecturer refers to) fill in the details, and modules 16–25 cover the decks that go beyond what has been lectured so far: expectation and variance, the named discrete distributions, continuous random variables, the normal, χ² and Student-t.

Every module opens with a READ FIRST panel that says in plain words what it is about. Then intuition, then the formula, then why the formula is stated the way it is. The figures with sliders are there to be played with — each one has preset buttons that reproduce the lecture's own numbers.

How to use: 01–09 = lecture 1 (sets, axioms, conditioning, independence). 10–15 = lecture 2 (Bayes, Monty Hall, random variables, the CDF, transformations, the pmf). 16–25 = ahead of the lectures, from the PDF decks. 26 = exam-style multiple choice (theory and R) plus an index of every example with its answer. The R corners throughout are there because a third of the exam is multiple choice on R.
