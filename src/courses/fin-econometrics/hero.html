Financial Econometrics — L. Mancini

The whole course, rebuilt to study from: every lecture, every derivation step by step, every exercise from the sheets and tutorials solved, and every open question on the slides answered. Each module opens with a READ FIRST panel in plain words, then definition, proof, figure, worked example, exercises with hidden solutions, and a quick test.

One small dataset of five observations runs through lectures 1–8, so that OLS, residuals, $R^2$, $s^2$, $t$ and $F$ are all computed by hand on the same numbers.

Order. 01 = map and notation. 02–05 = toolbox (skip what you know). 06–07 = lecture 1. 08–11 = lecture 2. 12–14 = lecture 3. 15–17 = lecture 4. 18–20 = lectures 5–6. 21–23 = lecture 7. 24–26 = lecture 8. 27–30 = time series. 31–35 = GARCH, VaR, ES. 36–40 = practice: all sheets, 14 proofs, three mock exams, a 30-question test with your scores, and the formula sheet.
