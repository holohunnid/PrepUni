TIP Exam routines— ⊡ ✕

Any limit

Substitute → number? done. $\frac c0$? $\pm\infty$, check sides. $\frac00$? factor / conjugate / L'Hôpital. $x\to\infty$? dominant power or growth hierarchy.

Any derivative

Simplify first (logs, roots as powers). Identify the outermost structure: sum → term by term; product → product rule; quotient → quotient rule; composition → chain rule and don't forget $\times g'$. Factor the answer.

Study a function / sketch a curve

Domain → intercepts → symmetry → asymptotes → $f'$ sign table → classify → $f''$ sign table → inflections → draw.

Max / min on $[a,b]$

Solve $f'=0$, add points where $f'$ is undefined, add both endpoints, evaluate $f$ at all of them, compare. No classification needed.

Any integral

Simplify → in the table? → inner function with its derivative present? substitution → product of unlike types? by parts (LIATE) → rational? divide then partial fractions → improper? write the limit explicitly. Differentiate your answer to check.

Any double integral

Sketch the region first. Find the intersection points. Choose vertical (Type I) or horizontal (Type II) slabs. Outer limits constant, inner may depend on the outer variable. If the inner integral is impossible, reverse the order.

Ten-second self-checks
Does the derivative of my antiderivative return the integrand? Is a positive integrand giving a positive answer? Did every $+C$ get written? Did the inner variable disappear after the inner integration? Is my "unit vector" actually a unit vector? Did I use radians?
