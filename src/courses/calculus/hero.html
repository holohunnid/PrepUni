Calculus Course

From "what is a function" to double integrals, written for someone who has met calculus before but never had it explained properly. Every idea arrives in the same order: the plain-language question it answers, the picture, then the formula, then a worked example. Nothing is asserted without a reason.

How to use: modules 1–3 are the foundations (functions, limits, continuity) — they are short, and skipping them is why calculus feels like arbitrary rules. Modules 4–6 build differentiation and what you do with it; 7–8 do integration; 9–10 extend both to several variables, which is where the syllabus is really aiming. Module 11 puts it into finance, module 12 is the night-before memory dump, and module 13 has 32 exam-style problems with full solutions — do them on paper before opening the answers.
