04

Determinants

one number that says whether a square matrix is invertible
DEF Computing det— ⊡ ✕

2×2

$$\det\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}=\begin{vmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{vmatrix}=ad-bc$$

3×3 (Sarrus rule)

$$\begin{vmatrix}a&b&c\\d&e&f\\g&h&i\end{vmatrix}=aei+bfg+cdh-ceg-bdi-afh$$

n×n: cofactor (Laplace) expansion

Along any row $i$ (or column $j$):

$$\det A=\sum_{j=1}^n a_{ij}\,C_{ij},\qquad C_{ij}=(-1)^{i+j}M_{ij}$$

$M_{ij}$ = minor = det of the $(n-1)\times(n-1)$ matrix after deleting row $i$ and column $j$. Sign pattern:

$$\begin{pmatrix}+&-&+\\-&+&-\\+&-&+\end{pmatrix}$$
Exam shortcut
Expand along the row/column with the most zeros. Better still: use row operations to create zeros first (rule 3 below leaves det unchanged), then expand. Triangular ⟹ det = product of the diagonal.

Geometric meaning

$|\det A|$ = factor by which $A$ scales area (2D) / volume (3D). Columns of $A$ span a parallelogram of area $|\det A|$. Sign negative ⟹ orientation flipped. $\det A=0$ ⟹ the columns are squashed into a lower dimension (dependent columns).

Cramer's rule (square, $\det A\neq0$)

$$x_i=\frac{\det A_i}{\det A}$$

$A_i$ = $A$ with column $i$ replaced by $\mathbf{b}$. Fine for 2×2 and 3×3, too slow beyond.

INTERACTIVE Determinant as signed area — drag the sliders— ⊡ ✕

$A=\begin{pmatrix}a&b\\c&d\end{pmatrix}$, columns $\mathbf{a}_1=\binom{a}{c}$ and $\mathbf{a}_2=\binom{b}{d}$.

  • Make the columns parallel (try the singular preset): the parallelogram collapses to a line, $\det=0$, $A$ is not invertible.
  • Swap the columns: area identical, sign flips.
  • Rotation: $\det=1$, area preserved, orientation preserved.
  • Double one column: det doubles (rule 2). Double both: det ×4 (rule 4 with $n=2$).
EX Worked example: 3×3 determinant two ways + Cramer— ⊡ ✕
$$A=\begin{pmatrix}2&1&-1\\-3&-1&2\\-2&1&2\end{pmatrix}$$

Sarrus

$$\begin{aligned}\det A&=2(-1)(2)+1(2)(-2)+(-1)(-3)(1)\\&\quad-(-1)(-1)(-2)-1(-3)(2)-2(2)(1)\\&=-4-4+3+2+6-4=\color{#3ea76a}{-1}\end{aligned}$$

Cofactor along row 1

$$\det A=2\begin{vmatrix}-1&2\\1&2\end{vmatrix}-1\begin{vmatrix}-3&2\\-2&2\end{vmatrix}+(-1)\begin{vmatrix}-3&-1\\-2&1\end{vmatrix}$$ $$=2(-4)-1(-2)-1(-5)=-8+2+5=\color{#3ea76a}{-1}\;\checkmark$$

Cramer for $x$ with $\mathbf{b}=(8,-11,-3)^{\mathsf T}$

$$A_1=\begin{pmatrix}\color{#4c8dff}{8}&1&-1\\\color{#4c8dff}{-11}&-1&2\\\color{#4c8dff}{-3}&1&2\end{pmatrix},\quad\det A_1=-2$$ $$x=\frac{\det A_1}{\det A}=\frac{-2}{-1}=2\;\checkmark\text{ (matches module 3)}$$
Trap
Signs in cofactor expansion follow the checkerboard, not the sign of the entry. Second most common error: forgetting that $\det(2A)=2^3\det A=8\det A$ for a 3×3.
