Refresher Course — Probability

This is the probability block of the USI Refresher Course in Mathematics and Probability. It follows the six lectures exactly — elements of set theory, elements of probability theory, conditional probability and independence, discrete random variables, continuous random variables, joint probability distributions and conditioning — and fills in everything the slides leave to the lecturer: why each definition is stated the way it is, what the formulas are actually saying, and how the six lectures fit together into one argument.

Every exercise that appears on a slide is here with a complete solution — including the several whose answer slide was left blank (the partition and De Morgan verifications in lecture 1, the joint-density and conditional-distribution problems in lecture 6, the Value at Risk and Expected Shortfall calculations). Where the lecturer gives an R command, the module also shows the arithmetic behind it, so you can reproduce the number by hand in an exam.

How to use: modules 01–06 are the foundations — sets, axioms, conditioning, independence — and they are short; do not skip them, because everything later is built on the two closure ideas and the three axioms. Modules 07–11 are discrete random variables (pmf, CDF, Bernoulli, binomial, Poisson, expectation, variance). Modules 12–13 redo the same story for continuous variables and the three named densities. Modules 14–16 handle two variables at once — joint distributions, covariance and correlation, conditioning. Module 17 applies all of it to VaR and expected shortfall. Module 18 collects all 76 exercises: the ten longest are worked there in full, and an index gives every other one its answer and the module that solves it.
